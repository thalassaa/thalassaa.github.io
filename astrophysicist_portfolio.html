About

I am a PhD student at the Institute of Astronomy of the Russian Academy of Sciences, specializing in stellar evolution and molecular astrophysics. My research focuses on understanding the complex chemical processes that occur during stellar evolution, particularly in hot molecular cores.

My work combines observational astronomy with advanced computational techniques, analyzing molecular radio emission to study the chemical composition and physical conditions in star-forming regions. I am particularly interested in how stellar evolution drives chemical enrichment in the interstellar medium.

In addition to my observational work, I am actively developing machine learning applications for astrophysical data analysis. This includes improving classification algorithms for astronomical objects, pattern recognition in spectral data, and automated analysis pipelines for large-scale surveys.

Research Interests

Stellar Evolution & Chemistry

Investigating the chemical processes that occur during stellar evolution, particularly focusing on how stars influence the chemical enrichment of the interstellar medium.

Hot Molecular Cores

Studying hot molecular cores through molecular radio emission analysis to understand their chemical composition, physical conditions, and evolutionary stages.

Molecular Radio Astronomy

Analyzing molecular radio emission data to characterize the physical and chemical properties of star-forming regions.

Machine Learning in Astrophysics

Developing and applying machine learning techniques to astrophysical data analysis, including automated classification and pattern recognition in spectral data.
